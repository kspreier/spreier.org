How recipients opt in

Program  AnchorPoint Gateway Alerts
Effective date  19 August 2026
Applies to  SMS notifications sent from the phone number ending 0785
Operated by  Kevin Spreier

This page exists so a reviewer can verify the consent flow directly. There is no public sign-up page to inspect, because there is no public sign-up — and there is no solicitation either. Every recipient asks to be added, in writing, on their own initiative.

01 Who receives these messages

At present, one person: the operator, about his own infrastructure. No other number is enrolled.

Anyone else is added only if they ask to be. There is no customer list, no marketing list, no purchased, rented or referred data, and consent is never obtained on anyone else's behalf.

02 How someone opts in

The recipient starts it, not the operator. The operator does not solicit consent, does not send an invitation, and does not add a number he has not been asked in writing to add. The complete flow is three steps:

1. The person emails the operator at sms@spreier.org asking to be added, and gives the mobile number in that email. For example: "Please add my number 555-555-0123 to the gateway alerts." 2. The operator puts that number into the system's configuration by hand. It is held in a machine-scope environment variable on the server, never in a file, in source code, or on a web form. 3. The change takes effect on the next deployment. Only then can any message be sent to that number.

There is no sign-up page, no web form, no join keyword, no QR code, and no paper form. Nothing is automated at any step: a human reads the request and a human edits the configuration.

03 How consent is recorded

The person's own request email is the record, retained by the operator with its date and the number they supplied. Because the recipient writes it themselves and states their own number, the request is the consent — there is no separate agreement to capture.

Consent is per person and specific to this program. It is never shared, transferred, or reused for any other purpose or sender.

04 How someone opts out

The same route, in reverse. The person emails the operator at sms@spreier.org asking to be removed; the operator deletes the number from the configuration, and it stops on the next deployment.

Independently of that, replying STOP (or CANCEL, QUIT, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, REVOKE, END) stops messages immediately at the carrier layer, which is required by US messaging rules and cannot be disabled by the operator. HELP or INFO returns assistance. Opting out is per person and affects no other recipient.

05 What is sent

Automated technical alerts about the operator's own trading-data infrastructure, sent only when a monitored system fails or recovers. Message frequency varies and is not fixed: nothing is sent while systems are healthy, and during an incident alerts repeat about every five minutes until it is resolved. Most weeks there are no messages at all. Message and data rates may apply.
